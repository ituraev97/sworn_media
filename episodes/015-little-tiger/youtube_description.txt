Little Tiger scammer sentenced: Jiandong Chen, the cash courier confronted by Trilogy Media in the viral "fake funeral" scambaiting video, gets 78 months in the $27 million elder fraud case.
Refund scam, tech support scam, scambaiters Trilogy Media and Scammer Payback, elder fraud, money mule, fake IDs, cryptocurrency laundering — the case explained from the U.S. Attorney's record.
Photo: DOJ sentencing slide published by the U.S. Attorney's Office, Southern District of California (Nov 13, 2026), U.S. government work.
On November 13, 2026, U.S. District Judge Robert S. Huie in San Diego sentenced Jiandong Chen, aka "Little Tiger," to 78 months (6½ years) in prison. Chen pleaded guilty in April 2026 to conspiracy to commit mail and wire fraud and conspiracy to launder money in a scheme that, according to the U.S. Attorney's Office, cost more than 2,000 elderly victims over $27 million in 2021–2023. He admitted his part involved over $9.5 million in victim losses. The ringleader, Zhao Wang ("Oscar"), was sentenced to 151 months in May 2026.
