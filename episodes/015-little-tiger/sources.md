## Claims → source
- July 2023 confrontation by Ashton Bingham and Art Kulik (Trilogy Media); both attended the sentencing → S. Chen went to a residence in the LA area to pick up bulk cash from someone he believed to be an elderly victim → S, CP.
- Video title, posted Aug 19, 2023, >1.4M views → YT; title also quoted in S and CP.
- Aka "Little Tiger"; age 40 Pomona CA (2024) → I; age 42 Los Angeles (2026) → CP, S. Case No. 24-cr-1317-RSH-02 → CP, S.
- Sentenced Nov 13, 2026 (Friday) to 6½ years / 78 months by U.S. District Judge Robert S. Huie → S.
- >2,000 elderly victims, >$27M losses, ~2021–2023, victims in their 70s and 80s → S, CP.
- Unsolicited calls/emails/pop-ups; India-based scam call centers (verbatim "Unbeknownst to the victims, those phone numbers belonged to India-based scam call centers."); tech support, bank, government impersonation, refund scams; remote desktop software → CP.
- Refund scam mechanics, "over-refunded", "In reality, the victims had not received any money." → CP; "scammed into sending their own money" → WS.
- Money sent by wire, bulk cash via express mail, in-person pickups → S.
- Fake names + retail locations incl. CVS Pharmacy; "money-laden packages using fake IDs" → I.
- Chen admitted fraudulent driver's licenses/IDs matching fictitious recipient names → CP.
- Wang ("Oscar"), lead defendant; couriers delivered money typically each day; crypto wallet from foreign co-conspirators; upwards of 18%; obtained fake IDs personally, recruited others incl. co-defendants → WP (also WS).
- Chen laundered via crypto to foreign counterparts, pay co-conspirators, fund travel incl. Las Vegas → S, CP.
- Government argued co-conspirators documented themselves retrieving/opening packages and counting money; "Agents seized countless such photos and videos from Wang's phone." → WS.
- Moved to U.S. Nov 2022; active by Feb 2023; package videos; same distinctive clothing as in viral video → S. Slides dated 2/22/2023, 2/23/2023 (CVS – 1302 W Mission Rd, San Marcos, CA), 5/19/2023 → S (slides).
- Arrested by local law enforcement June 2023; involvement continued through at least Dec 22, 2023 (federal agents, Upland CA, attempting to retrieve additional victim packages) → S.
- Plea agreement: >$9.5M victim loss, fake IDs, frequently relocated incl. to S.D. Cal. to evade law enforcement → S.
- Indictment June 2024 (CP: "charged by indictment in June 2024"); unsealed Jul 31, 2024; ~60 officers; four arrested in LA and Las Vegas, homes searched; fifth (Gong) already in state custody → I. (CP/WP say "In August 2024, federal agents conducted a nationwide takedown" — we use the Jul 31, 2024 release's own date.)
- Charges and maximum penalties (40 / 20 years) → I, CP, S.
- "According to the indictment, the defendants specifically targeted elderly Americans." → I.
- Advice quote (U.S. Attorney Tara McGrath, 2024) and National Elder Fraud Hotline 1-833-FRAUD-11 (1-833-372-8311), ic3.gov → I.
- Wang pleaded guilty Jan 15, 2026 → WP; Chen pleaded guilty Apr 2, 2026 → CP; Wang sentenced to 151 months (release title: 12.5 years) May 8, 2026 by Judge Huie → WS.
- Judge Huie (Wang): "staggering", "100 percent reprehensible", "Their vulnerability was not incidental. It was not coincidence. It was how they were selected. It was how they came to be scammed."; 83-year-old victim's wife and daughter; "It's really hard to fathom the depth of that harm in somebody's last months on this planet." → WS.
- $65M ring: 28 alleged members, four indictments, "Chinese organized crime ring", thousands of seniors, 97-year-old San Diego widow of a Holocaust survivor who lost her entire life savings; Scammer Payback (Pierogi) + Trilogy Media; videos 2020–2021 helped identify Zhiyi Zhang, Dudu Chen, Huajian Chen; "Hans Bum", South San Francisco, decoy package; Zhang revealed his identity and admitted on camera (per the government's detention memo); arrested Aug 19, 2025 at LAX; "Not all heroes wear capes. Some have YouTube channels." (U.S. Attorney Adam Gordon) → R.
- Investigation began Dec 2020 (elderly victim contacted express mail carrier; 11 packages, ~$135,000 cash, each addressed to a fake name and a short-term rental) ; hub-and-spoke rentals; last of 25 defendants admitted crimes (Friday before Oct 26, 2026); three charged defendants remain fugitives; Zhiyi Zhang and Huajian Chen admitted in plea agreements being in YouTube videos; Bing Shen sentenced 70 months; Na Rong sentenced 78 months → F.
- Judge Huie (Chen): "engaged in extensive course of conduct in stealing from some of the most vulnerable people here in the United States"; "more than just money"; "people's self-respect, their dignity, trust with their family members"; "desperation in the last years of somebody's life"; "tragic thing to suffer" → S.
- U.S. Attorney Adam Gordon: "These victims weren't foolish. They were lied to and exploited by a sophisticated criminal network." "Stopping fraud takes an online village. … with friends to our cause like Ash and Art, we can do even more." → S.
- Americans 60+ reported >$7.7B losses in 2025 → IC3 (via Bitdefender).
