Earl Thomas fraud case explained: on November 12, 2026, Nina Thomas, the estranged wife of former Seahawks safety Earl Thomas, and her boyfriend Kevin J. Thompson pleaded guilty in federal court in New Orleans to conspiracy to commit wire fraud. The Justice Department says they stole between $1.5 million and $3 million from the victim, using accounts opened in his name; Thompson also pleaded guilty to a cocaine count.

The Justice Department's releases do not name the victim; news reports since 2023, citing the Jefferson Parish Sheriff's Office and court records, identify him as Earl Thomas. Both defendants pleaded guilty before U.S. District Judge Brandon S. Long (E.D. La.); sentencing is pending.
