# Sources — Episode 006: Bernard Ebbers / WorldCom (THE PAPER TRAIL)

## Primary documents (shown on screen)
- **Brief for the United States in Opposition**, Ebbers v. United States, No. 06-590 (Solicitor General Paul D. Clement, filed Jan 29, 2007) — archive.org item `micro_IA40386016_1666`, file "2. Opposition Brief.pdf": https://archive.org/details/micro_IA40386016_1666
  - line costs = largest expense; dot-com bust; Q3 2000 line costs "almost $1 billion higher than expected" (p. 2)
  - ~$3.8 billion moved from line-cost expense to capital-expenditure accounts, Q1 2001–Q1 2002 (p. 3)
  - "hitting our numbers"; 14 witnesses, 300+ exhibits; five cooperators incl. Sullivan (p. 4)
  - Ebbers testified; denied speaking with Sullivan about improper adjustments; "never noticed"; trash testimony (pp. 4–5)
  - conscious-avoidance instruction text: "whether [petitioner] deliberately closed his eyes to what otherwise would have been obvious" (p. 6); "Based in large part on petitioner's own testimony"
- **U.S. v. Ebbers, 458 F.3d 110 (2d Cir. Jul 28, 2006)** (Winter, J.) — Appendix A of the cert petition, same archive.org item
  - teacher, coach, motel operator; LDDS 1983; CEO 1985; WorldCom 1995; MCI 1998; ~90,000 employees, 65 countries, $39B revenues (3a)
  - borrowed over $400M, margin calls, $70.5M forward sale, WorldCom assumed liability Oct 2000 (4a–5a)
  - Sullivan urged earnings warning, Ebbers refused; "[W]e have to hit our numbers." (5a)
  - Q3 2000 reserves → ~$828M; Vinson & Normand considered resigning; Ebbers' remark and apology to Myers (6a)
  - Q4 2000: $407M tax reserve, $797M; MBVRs 78% Sept 2000, 74% Dec 2000 (7a)
  - Q1 2001 $771M capitalized; March 2001 Washington dinner, "over $500 million — 'wasn't right.'"; "no storms on the horizon", "go out and buy stock" (8a)
  - Close the Gap; voicemail "accounting fluff," "one-time stuff," "junk" (9a); "lifeblood was in the stock", ~$12/share; Q2 2001 over $610M (9a)
  - Q3 2001 over $700M (10a); Q4 2001 over $941M; "[w]e stand by our accounting" (11a); Q1 2002 about $818M (11a)
  - SEC investigation March 2002; resigned April 29; bought 3M shares May 2002; Internal Audit; Sullivan fired; disclosed June 25, 2002; stock lost 90%; bankruptcy; superseding indictment Sept 15, 2004 (nine counts); verdict March 15, 2005; guidelines 30-to-life; 25 years, 3 yrs supervised release, no fine (12a–13a)
  - "using a procedure for signing documents he didn't bother to read in full, including the 10-Ks, and tossing the management budget variance report in the trash without reading it." (30a)
  - co-defendant sentences: Sullivan 5 yrs; Myers 1 yr 1 day; Yates 1 yr 1 day; Vinson 5 months; Normand 3 yrs probation; all cooperated and pled guilty, each a subordinate (38a–39a)
  - "Twenty-five years is a long sentence for a white collar crime"; "the sentence is harsh but not unreasonable" (39a–41a)
- **Order, U.S. v. Ebbers, S4 02 Cr. 1144 (BSJ), S.D.N.Y., Jul 9, 2005** (Judge Barbara S. Jones) — Appendix B, same item: September 2000 MBVR line costs $732M vs. $1.4B budget; "did not notice" (51a–52a); reports "in the trash" (54a); Cynthia Cooper among witnesses (62a)
- **DOJ Corporate Fraud Task Force, First Year Report to the President (Jul 22, 2003)**: https://www.justice.gov/archive/dag/cftf/first_year_report.pdf
  - p. 2.5: "the largest accounting fraud in U.S. history"; criminal complaint against former CFO and Controller on Aug 1, 2002, "five weeks after the initial revelation"; conviction of former Controller and three former members of General Accounting Department; $750 million to victim-investors
  - p. 3.26: within 24 hours SEC filed action, accounting fraud "totaling more than $3.8 billion"; $2.25B penalty satisfied by $500M cash + $250M stock
- **Government letter opposing compassionate release, Oct 4, 2019 (Dkt. 354)**: https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/page/file/1216821/dl
  - trial Jan 24–Mar 15, 2005; stock down >90% after June 25, 2002; sentencing July 13, 2005; Ebbers 63; loss over $2 billion "a reasonable estimate"; "instigator of the fraud"; 30 years to life; quotes of Judge Jones; no fine, no restitution (class settlement); surrendered Sept 26, 2006; release date July 4, 2028; motion Sept 5, 2019; conditions listed (macular degeneration/legally blind, cardiomyopathy, weight loss); Judge Jones letter "has been punished enough"; "nearly 13 years"
  - Exhibit B = sentencing transcript, Jul 13, 2005 (pp. 37, 60–61 of transcript): "Although I recognize, as the defense has pointed out, that this sentence is likely to be a life sentence for Mr. Ebbers, I find that a sentence of anything less would not sufficiently reflect the seriousness of this crime."; 25-year structure; restitution covered by class settlement (quoted only, not shown as image)
- **Government letter, Oct 23, 2019 (Dkt. 358)**: https://www.justice.gov/usao-sdny/page/file/1216831/dl (health status update; not used on screen)
