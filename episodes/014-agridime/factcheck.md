Notes on the items marked OK that you asked about:
- **"BREAKING" tag**: verdict Tue Nov 10, 2026 (Nov 10, 2026 is a Tuesday); publish Nov 15 = 5 days. OK.
- **"SOLD COWS IT DIDN'T HAVE"** is a fair plain-English version of the DOJ wording ("sold over approximately 60,000 heads of cattle to the public than they controlled in their cattle inventory"). Every "about 60,000 more head … than it controlled" phrasing (episode lines 2, 18, 62 and the summary) matches the release. The chart numbers 115,255 and 51,704 match exhibit KC15 (pr_img2); 115,255 − 51,704 = 63,551, which fits "over approximately 60,000". The only phrasing that is not fair is "Only 51,704 Bought" (FIX 15): the exhibit's figure is *net* cattle purchased.
- **"Ponzi"**: episode lines 3 and 58 attribute it to the U.S. Attorney (exact quote). Lines 19 and 45 attribute it to the SEC (exact quotes). The title and thumbnail B use it without attribution. That is acceptable after the verdict: the release says trial evidence proved that buyer funds paid "funds owed by Agridime to earlier cattle purchasers", and the U.S. Attorney used the word.
- **Royana Thomas pitch (lines 31–32)**: SSI p.10 ¶17(c)(ii) reads "….where we really make the money on this…. whole operation is that we also have a meat distributing company… where we end up making the money is when we harvest, process and get the meat out… we sell to whole, big wholesalers and retail…. and that's why we're able to offer that percentage… because of the yield." The say/show (with "…" marking the omission) and the q field ("where we end up making the money is when we harvest, process and get the meat out") are verbatim. "24.44%", the Nov 28, 2023 date, "via telephone", and "his" (a male buyer) all match. Thomas's own factual resume ¶11 stipulates the call and that the statement was false, so "The indictment calls that false" is, if anything, an understatement.
- **"Individual TV"** = the Agridime Receiver, appointed Dec 11, 2023 in SEC case 4:23-cv-01224-P (SSI ¶38). In the Mar 11 indictment the same person is "Individual A". Line 42 calls this person "the court-appointed receiver" and does not name them. OK.
- **Victims**: none are named anywhere. OK.
- **Salary table (line 21)**: pr_img1 covers 2021–2023, with Josh & Tia at $2,728,628 (rounds to $2.73M) and Wood at $2,414,628 (rounds to $2.41M). The figures and the year span are OK. The release *text* says "from 2022 through 2023", but the table is the better source. Only the "government exhibit" label is unsupported (FIX 4).

## FIX list

**FIX 1 — episode.json line 2 (say/show)**
- Current show: "Prosecutors say their company sold cattle to more than 2,200 Americans, and collected over $240 million. …"
- Problem: "their company" is wrong for Jason Link. Agridime was co-owned by Joshua Link and Jed Wood. Jason owned Shady Brook Ranch and was an Agridime broker and Meat Chain Director (verdict release; SSI ¶¶2–3, 7). Also, the release says the victims were "located throughout the United States", not that they were "Americans".
- Replace show: "Prosecutors say Joshua Link's company sold cattle to more than 2,200 people across the U.S., and collected over $240 million. It sold about 60,000 more head of cattle than it controlled."
- Replace say: "Prosecutors say Joshua Link's company sold cattle to more than twenty-two hundred people across the U.S., and collected over two hundred forty million dollars. It sold about sixty thousand more head of cattle than it controlled."
