Jordan Belfort restitution explained: in 2003 a federal judge ordered the real Wolf of Wall Street to repay $110,362,993.87 to his Stratton Oakmont victims. By 2018 prosecutors said he had paid about $12.8 million and still owed about $97 million. In 2026 TMZ reported his obligation expired under the 20-year federal limit.
