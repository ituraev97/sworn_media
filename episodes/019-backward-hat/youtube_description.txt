Backward Hat Capital explained: on November 18, 2026, federal prosecutors in Manhattan unsealed a criminal complaint charging two of its three co-founders, Phillip Patrick Williams and Devin Allen Johnson, with securities fraud and wire-fraud and securities-fraud conspiracy. The complaint alleges the forex fund raised more than $600 million from over 2,000 investors with reported returns that typically exceeded 6% a month, paid earlier investors with new money, and sent more than $130 million to Williams. Both men are presumed innocent.
