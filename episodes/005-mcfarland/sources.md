# Sources — SWORN Case 005: United States v. William McFarland (S.D.N.Y. 17 Cr. 600 (NRB); 18 Cr. 446) · SEC v. McFarland et al. (S.D.N.Y. 2018)

Court filings come from the RECAP mirror on the Internet Archive (items gov.uscourts.nysd.481447 and gov.uscourts.nysd.496262). Local copies used for research: /home/claude/research/mcf/pdf, text in /home/claude/research/mcf/txt (pdftotext -layout) and OCR in /home/claude/research/mcf/ocr. Images in img/ are rasterized from government-authored filings and court orders only (pdftoppm -r 220) by make_img.py / manual_boxes.py. The DOJ letterhead seal on the bail letter is cropped out (crop starts at 15% of the page). Exhibits written by private parties (emails, employee letter, victim statements) are NOT shown as images; where quoted, the cite says "As quoted in the Government's Sentencing Memorandum". No private victim, employee, driver or contractor is named. Court-reporter transcripts are quoted in quote cards only, never shown as images.
