Why did Sunny Balwani get more prison time than Elizabeth Holmes? Same Theranos indictment, same judge, same 135-to-168-month sentencing range — yet Holmes got 135 months and Ramesh "Sunny" Balwani, Theranos's former president and COO, got 155. Holmes's jury acquitted her of defrauding patients; Balwani's jury convicted him on all 12 counts, investors and patients. On October 5, 2026, the Supreme Court declined to hear his case.
The other Theranos verdict, from the court record: the SEC complaint over the financial projections he drafted (as alleged), the "null protocol" demos, the lab warnings and his "disaster zone" text, the $1,126,661 ad buy that became Count 12, the July 7, 2022 verdict, the 155-month sentence, $452 million in restitution, the Ninth Circuit's ruling and the denied Supreme Court petition.
