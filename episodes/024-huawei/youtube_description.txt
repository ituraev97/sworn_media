Huawei verdict: on November 24, 2026, a federal jury in Brooklyn convicted Huawei Technologies and three subsidiaries — Huawei Device, Futurewei and Huawei Device USA — of conspiracy to steal trade secrets, conspiracy to commit wire and bank fraud, wire fraud, bank fraud, conspiracy to defraud the United States, conspiracy to violate and violations of the International Emergency Economic Powers Act (Iran sanctions), and money laundering conspiracy, the Justice Department announced. According to AFP, Huawei was convicted on 10 of 12 charges and acquitted of racketeering conspiracy and conspiracy to obstruct justice. This video walks through the evidence the government says it presented, from a T-Mobile test robot's arm to Cisco's code, a company called Skycom that the indictment calls Huawei's unofficial subsidiary in Iran, and the statements to banks that the Justice Department says were false, using the superseding indictment, the government's letters to the judge, and the DOJ announcement. Sentencing is set for September 14, 2027. Huawei rejects the remaining allegations and, as reported by AFP, its attorney says it expects to appeal.
