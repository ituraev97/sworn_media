Why did Martin Shkreli go to prison? Not for raising the price of Daraprim from $17.60 to $750 a tablet — none of the criminal charges were about the pill. "Pharma Bro" Martin Shkreli was convicted of securities fraud for lying to investors in his hedge funds, and of conspiring to commit securities fraud involving the stock of his drug company Retrophin. He told investors MSMB Capital managed about $35 million when, a federal judge wrote, it never held more than about $1.13 million at one time.
The paper trail from the court record: the Brooklyn indictment, the jury's split verdict, the bail revocation, the 7-year sentence and the forfeited Wu-Tang Clan album — then the FTC's Daraprim case, a recorded call from prison, and the 2022 lifetime ban from the pharmaceutical industry with $64.6 million in disgorgement, upheld on appeal in 2024.
