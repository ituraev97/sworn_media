Court filings come from the RECAP mirror on the Internet Archive. Images in img/ are rasterized from these filings (pdftoppm -r 220) or rendered from the archived justice.gov release pages (images disabled, so no seal), by make_img.py (crop boxes listed there). No seals or logos appear in the crops. No private victim is named on screen or in narration; the brief names victim-depositors on pp. 9, 31 — those pages are not shown.
