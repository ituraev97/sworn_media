## #5 Scott Rothstein (S.D. Fla. 0:09-cr-60331)
- [RR_SENT] USAO S.D. Fla., Jun 9, 2010 — https://www.justice.gov/archive/usao/fls/PressReleases/2010/100609-01.html — sentenced to 50 years by Judge James I. Cohn; Jan 27, 2010 plea to RICO conspiracy, money-laundering conspiracy, mail/wire-fraud conspiracy, two counts of wire fraud (5 counts); "operation of a $1.2 billion Ponzi scheme through the defunct Ft. Lauderdale law firm Rothstein Rosenfeldt and Adler"; around 2005 through November 2009; settlements "could be purchased at a discount and would be repaid over time to the investors at full face value"; "funds would be held in the trust account of RRA"; "In both instances, the purported investment vehicles never existed"; false bank documents, false on-line bank account information, false settlement agreements and promissory notes; "Without the clients' knowledge, RRA settled the lawsuit in favor of the defendant"; "created a false federal court order, purportedly signed by a U.S. District Judge … owed a judgment of approximately $23 million"; clients required to post bonds; "wire transfer approximately $57 million to a trust account he controlled". (img/rr_head, rr_never, rr_order)
- [RR_PLEA] USAO S.D. Fla., Jan 27, 2010 — https://www.justice.gov/archive/usao/fls/PressReleases/2010/100127-01.html — five-count Information; "fraudulently obtained approximately $1.2 billion from investors"; 2005 through November 2009.
- [RR_AP] Associated Press via Washington Times, Apr 16, 2018 — https://m.washingtontimes.com/news/2018/apr/16/judge-refuses-to-reduce-ponzi-schemers-50-year-sen/ — Judge Cohn granted prosecutor's motion to withdraw an earlier offer to reduce the sentence; prosecutors said Rothstein "failed to comply with his plea agreement" by providing false information; he cooperated extensively after voluntarily returning from Morocco in 2009.

## #4 Tom Petters (D. Minn. 0:08-cr-00364, RECAP gov.uscourts.mnd.103973)
- [PT_AFF] Dkt. 26, Criminal Complaint + Affidavit of FBI SA Dan Harris, Oct 2, 2008 — https://archive.org/download/gov.uscourts.mnd.103973/gov.uscourts.mnd.103973.26.0.pdf — p.3 ¶7 "A Cooperating Witness … approached the government"; ¶7d "The purchase orders and other documents in support of the transactions are entirely fabricated"; p.5 ¶9a "The Wal-Mart official reported that PCI's purported vendor number on the Sam's Club purchase order was fictitious"; p.6 ¶12a "PETTERS repeatedly admits executing the fraud scheme … PETTERS talks about fleeing the country and creating fabricated defenses if the fraud scheme is discovered." (img/pet_cw, pet_wm, pet_rec)
- [PT_PR] USAO D. Minn., Apr 8, 2010 — https://www.justice.gov/sites/default/files/usao-mn/legacy/2010/12/21/econ0413.pdf — Petters, 52, of Wayzata; 50 years; "longest term of imprisonment ever ordered in a financial fraud case in Minnesota history"; Judge Kyle: "It just didn't pass the smell test" (referring to the lack of believability in Petters's trial testimony); convicted Dec 2, 2009 of 10 wire fraud, 3 mail fraud, 1 conspiracy, 1 money-laundering conspiracy, 5 money laundering; funds "to purchase merchandise that was to be resold to retailers at a profit. However, no such purchases were made."; Sept 8, 2008 Deanna Coleman and her attorney reported to authorities; electronic goods for big-box retailers such as Costco and Sam's Club; she wore a recording device; "Within the first few hours … Petters was heard admitting that purchase orders were 'fake' and claiming 'divine intervention' was the only explanation for how he and his co-conspirators 'could of got away with this for so long.'"; Sept 24, 2008 search warrants at headquarters, home; arrested Oct 3 after discussing fleeing; account only Petters and Coleman authorized to use; Jan 2003–Sept 2008 approx. $35 billion wired in; "none of the deposits into the account came from retailers"; Fingerhut, Polaroid, Sun Country Airlines purchased with proceeds of the PCI fraud; Coleman hired as office manager 1993. (img/pet_pr, pet_flow)
- [PT_GOV] Dkt. 388, Government's Position Regarding Sentencing, Mar 8, 2010 — https://archive.org/download/gov.uscourts.mnd.103973/gov.uscourts.mnd.103973.388.0.pdf — life sentence → "maximum statutory sentence permitted by the counts of conviction (335 years)". (img/pet_gov)
- [PT_JUD] Dkt. 400, Judgment, Apr 8, 2010 — https://archive.org/download/gov.uscourts.mnd.103973/gov.uscourts.mnd.103973.400.0.pdf — found guilty on counts 1–20 after a plea of not guilty; "total term of 600 months - 50 years". (img/pet_judg — page 2 only; page 1 not shown)
- [VERDICT] Dkt. 361, Jury Verdict, Dec 2, 2009 (docket entry: guilty on counts 1s–20s). Not shown (names a private lender).
- [PT_APP] KARE 11 — https://kare11.com/article/news/local/petters-conviction-upheld-by-federal-appeals-court/89-359186356 — Eighth Circuit upheld conviction and 50-year sentence; Star Tribune — https://m.startribune.com/supreme-court-won-t-pick-up-petters-appeal/151542975/ — Supreme Court denied review of conviction and sentence. Docket Dkt. 628 (Dec 5, 2013): order denying § 2255 motion.
- FBI Minneapolis release, Apr 8, 2010 — https://www.fbi.gov/minneapolis/press-releases/2010/mp040810.htm (confirms 50 years, $3.7 billion, Dec 2, 2009, Kyle quotes).

## #3 Edward H. Okun (E.D. Va. 3:08-cr-00132, RECAP gov.uscourts.vaed.227739)
- [OK_REL] USAO E.D. Va., Nov 17, 2011 — https://www.justice.gov/archive/usao/vae/news/2011/11/20111117okunnr.html — Fourth Circuit upheld conviction and 100-year sentence; owner of The 1031 Tax Group LLP; "loss of more than $126 million in client funds"; MacBride quote "Financial fraudsters make calculated, rational decisions, and the threat of spending as much as 100 years in prison can begin to change corporate culture and behavior"; convicted Mar 19, 2009 (Richmond) of conspiracy to commit mail and wire fraud, wire fraud, conspiracy to commit money laundering, money laundering, bulk cash smuggling and perjury; sentenced Aug 4, 2009 by Judge Robert E. Payne to 100 years; Section 1031 explanation; clients promised funds used "solely to effect 1031 exchanges"; "misappropriated approximately $126 million in client funds to support his lavish lifestyle, pay operating expenses for his various companies, invest in commercial real estate, and purchase additional qualified intermediary companies to obtain access to additional client funds"; $15,000 cash smuggled to "his personal yacht on Paradise Island in the Bahamas to avoid federal currency reporting requirements".
