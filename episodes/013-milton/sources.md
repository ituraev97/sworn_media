## Primary documents
- Government's Sentencing Memorandum, Dkt. 315 (Dec 12, 2023) — https://archive.org/details/gov.uscourts.nysd.564175 — Nikola One unveiling Dec 1–2, 2016, broadcast on the internet; "neither functioning nor even designed to be hydrogen-powered and was missing critical parts"; infotainment screens "nothing more than static mockups saved to laptop lock screens"; commercial shoot, truck "rolled down a hill"; Milton "became irate", "going down the road as we talked about", "was the reason why I approved it" (GX 925); "[t]his will be the first time anyone has seen the Nikola truck move … so it could get millions of views on our youtube channel" (GX 929); "In Motion Video" posted on Twitter, YouTube, Facebook; market cap "higher than that of Ford's, despite Nikola never having produced a vehicle for sale at the time"; CFO Kim Brady testimony "one of [Milton's] goal[s] was to be in the top 100" of Forbes list (Trial Tr. 1770); holdings $1.099B (Mar 2020) → $7.303B peak (Jun 2020) (GX 1001); sold ~$100M May–June 2020; spent ~$83.5M by Sept 2020 incl. airplane, hangar, Turks & Caicos estate; Badger "from the ground up" vs reworked Ford prototypes / "GM trucks with Nikola branding"; hydrogen "producing … when it was not", below $4/kg "when it had not"; firm contracts worth billions "That too was false"; "warned over and over again"; ranch: $8.5M cash + options valued $8.5M; April 10, 2020 call with seller and son; "we've already begun procuring power and planning stations…" (GX 1400-T) / "None of these claims were true" (p.23); Hindenburg Research report Sept 10–11, 2020 "detailing evidence of the defendant's fraud"; resignation announced Sept 21, 2020; market cap $16B (Sept 9) → $14.19B (Sept 10) → $12.13B (Sept 11); loss $660.8–$673.6M; Guidelines 60 years; Probation recommends 132 months, Government in line with it; defense asked probation, loss zero; Holmes 135 months compared; thousands of victims.
- Opinion and Order, Dkt. 286 (Aug 30, 2023) — indictment filed July 28, 2021; guilty on three of four counts, not guilty on Count Two (18 U.S.C. § 1348); company went public June 2020, Milton executive chairman and largest shareholder; trial Sept 13 – Oct 14, 2022; "four big categories" of alleged lies; Milton's new-trial motion (jury instructions, Juror No. 6) — DENIED.
- Opinion & Order, Dkt. 334 (Feb 26, 2024) — 48 months concurrent, 3 yrs supervised release, $1M fine; Wasatch Creeks Ranch 4,700 acres; forfeiture objections denied; stay of forfeiture "and all other financial aspects of the sentence" pending appeal GRANTED.
- Judgment, Dkt. 327 (Jan 17, 2024; OCR) — guilty counts 1, 3, 4 of S1 indictment; not guilty count 2; "48 months on counts 1, 3, and 4, to run concurrently."
- Sentencing transcript, Dkt. 322 (Dec 18, 2023) — quoted only: Milton "I told the truth." (p.73); judge on loss "There was real loss to real people who listened to Mr. Milton and invested in Nikola because of what they heard from him" (p.21); Holmes distinction "That's not what Mr. Milton did, in my view." (p.91); "I believe the jury got it right." (p.92); "The law does not grant a pass for good intentions" (p.93); victim letter read by judge, truck driver, "I watched a video of a Nikola truck appearing to drive effortlessly." (p.94); sentence 48 months, $1 million fine, forfeiture of Wasatch ranch, restitution within 90 days (p.89); bail pending appeal granted, "I will allow him to remain free pending appeal."
- Government restitution letter, Dkt. 361 (Mar 14, 2025) — sentenced Dec 18, 2023 to 48 months; asks restitution of $660,800,000 to Nikola shareholders.
- Joint status letter, Dkt. 362 (Apr 1, 2025) + Exhibit A Executive Grant of Clemency — March 27, 2025 "A Full and Unconditional Pardon", "remission of any and all fines, penalties, forfeitures, and restitution ordered by the court"; Ex parte Garland quote "blots out of existence the guilt"; parties agree new-trial motion and restitution request moot; appeal to be dismissed as moot.
- Order Exonerating Bail, Dkt. 365 (Apr 7, 2025) — bail exonerated; $300 special assessment refunded.
- Indictment, Dkt. 1 (Jul 28, 2021; OCR) — ¶1 scheme Nov 2019–Sept 2020, "retail investors", social media, television, print, podcast interviews.
- SEC v. Milton Complaint (Jul 29, 2021) — ALLEGATIONS: chief engineer warned truck would not be functioning unless event postponed (¶52); "fully functions and works which is really incredible" (¶53); commercial shoot for "a leading supplier and manufacturer of parts for the trucking industry", "could not run under its own power", "towed to the top of an inclined stretch of road and then filmed rolling down the incline", repeated three times (¶60); Jan 25, 2018 tweet "Behold, the Nikola One in motion." drafted by Milton (¶62–63); posted until at least Sept 2020 (¶65); "billions and billions of dollars with the contracts" (¶105); reservations non-binding except 800-truck order (¶108).
- SEC Joint Stipulation to Dismiss, Dkt. 53 (Sep 11, 2025; OCR) — "in the exercise of its discretion, the Commission believes the dismissal of this case is appropriate."
