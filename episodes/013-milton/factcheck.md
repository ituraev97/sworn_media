# SWORN 013 — United States v. Trevor Milton — independent fact-check

Checked against the local sources in /home/claude/src (gm315, op286, op334, tr322, sec_complaint, d361, d362/d362.1, d365, jdg327, secd53, indictment_ocr) and the four web sources (DOJ press release, AP verdict, AP pardon, eWEEK; WebFetch).

Items checked: 66 episode lines (say, show and all scene fields) + 12 short lines + 3 short titles/hooks + thumb (2 variants) + meta title and summary = **85 items**. **22 FIXes** (marked FIX).

General notes:
- The private ranch seller is redacted in the gm_10 and rest images and is not named anywhere in the text. Kim Brady and Mark Russell (Nikola executives) are visible in the gm_7 and gm_8 images. They are corporate officers, not private victims, so this is acceptable.
- Printed memo page numbers: gm_6 = p.4, gm_7 = p.5, gm_8 = p.6, gm_9 = p.7, gm_10 = p.8, gm_pre = p.1.
- The judge's own Guidelines calculation at sentencing was offense level 41, a range of 324–405 months (tr322 p.25–26). The "60 years" figure is the government's calculation (offense level 43, capped at the statutory maximum). The episode attributes it to "the government's math", so that is acceptable.
