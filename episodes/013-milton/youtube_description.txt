How was Nikola founder Trevor Milton convicted of fraud? The Nikola One truck in the famous "in motion" video was not driving — according to the SEC, it was towed up an incline and filmed rolling down. In 2022 a Manhattan jury convicted Milton of securities fraud and wire fraud; he was sentenced to four years, stayed free on bail pending appeal, and in March 2025 received a full and unconditional presidential pardon.
