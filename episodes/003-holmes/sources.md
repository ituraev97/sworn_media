# Sources — SWORN Case 003: United States v. Elizabeth A. Holmes (N.D. Cal. 5:18-cr-00258-EJD)

Court filings retrieved from the RECAP mirror of the N.D. Cal. docket on the Internet Archive (https://archive.org/download/gov.uscourts.cand.327949/gov.uscourts.cand.327949.<Dkt>.0.pdf). Images in img/ are rasterized from these filings (pdftoppm -r 220) by make_img.py (crop boxes listed there). No seals/logos appear in the crops. No private victim is named on screen or in narration (the restitution list and victim-impact passages were cropped out).

- [TSI] Third Superseding Indictment, Dkt. 469, Jul 28, 2020 — two conspiracy counts (investors, patients) + ten wire fraud counts; "null protocol" ¶(C) p.6; counts 3–8 wire table: Count 6 2/6/2014 $38,336,632; Count 7 10/31/2014 $99,999,984; Count 8 10/31/2014 $5,999,997 (img/tsi_cover, tsi_null, tsi_counts)
- [VF] Final verdict form, Dkt. 1235, Jan 3, 2022 — Count 1 Guilty; Count 2 Not Guilty; Count 3 blank; Counts 6, 7, 8 Guilty; Counts 10, 11 (and 12) Not Guilty (img/verdict1, verdict2)
- [MIN1] Minute order, Dkt. 1236, Jan 3, 2022 — mistrial as to Counts 3, 4, 5
- [DOJV] U.S. Attorney N.D. Cal. release, Jan 4, 2022 — https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndca/pr/theranos-founder-elizabeth-holmes-found-guilty-investor-fraud — convicted of conspiracy + 3 wire fraud counts; acquitted on patient conspiracy + 3 wire counts; one patient count dismissed during trial; hung on 3 investor counts; FBI SAC Craig D. Fair: "Elizabeth Holmes chose fraud over business failure."
- [DOJC] U.S. Attorney N.D. Cal. case page — https://www.justice.gov/usao-ndca/us-v-elizabeth-holmes-et-al — indictment Jun 14, 2018; Holmes sentenced Nov 18, 2022 to 135 months; Balwani sentenced Dec 7, 2022 to 155 months (12 years, 11 months)
- [MEMO] Government sentencing memorandum, Dkt. 1643, Nov 11, 2022 — p.6 "she has a promise to deliver to the customer…" (Tr. 1216); "At trial, she blamed her COO…everyone, that is, but herself."; p.7 recommends 180 months; the pitch (one device, any test, validated by pharma, DoD battlefield, profitable); p.8 device could never complete more than 12 types of blood tests; third-party (Siemens) machines; zero revenue 2012 and 2013; April 2010 reports with Pfizer/GSK/Schering-Plough logos, "three independent due diligence reports on Theranos systems"; Holmes admitted adding logos; kept sending doctored Pfizer report to investors 2014–2015; p.9 projections $140M (2014) and nearly $1B (2015); Dec 2013 claim of >$200M from DoD and pharma vs. never more than $10M combined; scientists warned Aug–Sept 2013, resigned shortly before Walgreens launch; medevac helicopters/battlefield claims; DoD never used device to clinically treat patients; p.12 Sept 22, 2015 texts "Our validation reports are terrible." / "Praying"; shares valued at more than $4 billion by end of 2014; covers of Fortune, Forbes, Inc., Glamour, T; Time 100 Most Influential; p.17–18 CMS 2015 inspection; Theranos admitted "a global and long-term failure of the quality control program for this instrument"; voided every patient test result ever run on Edison in clinical lab; p.18 hired David Boies; appealed to WSJ owner (a significant investor); p.19 Oct 15, 2015 Mad Money: "every test that we offer in our laboratory can run on our proprietary devices."; p.20 trial began Aug 31, 2021, four months, >900 exhibits, 32 witnesses; Balwani guilty of all twelve counts; PSR recommends 108 months
- [DEF] Defense sentencing memorandum, Dkt. 1642, Nov 10, 2022 — "a term of eighteen months or less"; "home confinement … is sufficient"
- [MIN] Criminal minutes — sentencing, Dkt. 1660, Nov 18, 2022 — 135 months on Counts 1, 6, 7, 8 concurrent; 3 years supervised release; $400 assessment; no fine; surrender Apr 27, 2023; recommendation FPC Bryan, Texas
- [SO] Order on sentencing, Dkt. 1712, Jan 10, 2023 — PSR loss $730,840,209 across 29 investors; stock not "practically worthless"; loss $120,146,247; apologized for Theranos and its "failings" but did not accept responsibility; 135 months
- [JUDG] Judgment, Dkt. 1716, Jan 11, 2023 — guilty Counts 1, 6–8; not guilty Counts 2, 10–12; Counts 3–5 and 9 dismissed on motion of the United States
- [BAIL] Order denying release pending appeal, Dkt. 1757, Apr 10, 2023
- [REST] Order on restitution, Dkt. 1760, May 16, 2023 — $452,047,268, joint and several with Balwani
- [SURR] Order setting reporting date May 30, 2023, Dkt. 1762
- [CA9] Ninth Circuit, U.S. v. Holmes, Nos. 22-10312 et al., filed Feb 24, 2025, amended Dec 22, 2025 (Dkt. 1790, 1802) — "[O]ne tiny drop changes everything."; claimed fast, accurate, affordable tests with a drop of blood from the prick of a finger; "nothing more than a mirage"; "half-truths and outright lies"; Holmes founded Theranos 2003, CEO until 2018; Walgreens partnership 2010, launch fall 2013; Nov 2013 down to $15M cash, spending up to $2M weekly; 2015 total income $1,944,948 reported to IRS; "null protocol" quote; Holmes admitted affixing logos; company witnesses neither validated nor authorized logos; affirmed convictions, sentences, $452M restitution; news reporting late 2015
- [RS] Order granting motion for modification of sentence, Dkt. 1816, Mar 26, 2026 — Amendment 821 (zero-point offenders); restitution to 12 investor-victims incl. Walgreens and Safeway; began serving May 30, 2023; Ninth Circuit affirmed Feb 24, 2025; rehearing denied May 8, 2025; resentenced to 123 months; "To be clear, this sentence reduction does not diminish the enormity of Holmes's crimes"
- [SEC] SEC press release 2018-41, Mar 14, 2018 — https://www.sec.gov/news/press-release/2018-41 — "massive fraud"; more than $700 million raised; Holmes: $500,000 penalty, 10-year officer/director bar, return 18.9 million shares, give up voting control; Steven Peikin: "Investors are entitled to nothing less than complete truth and candor from companies and their executives"
