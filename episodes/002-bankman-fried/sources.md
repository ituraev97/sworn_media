# Sources — SWORN Case 002: United States v. Samuel Bankman-Fried (S.D.N.Y. 22 Cr. 673 (LAK))

Court filings were retrieved from the RECAP archive of the S.D.N.Y. docket (PACER documents mirrored on the Internet Archive). Images in img/ are rasterized from these filings (pdftoppm -r 220); crop script: make_img.py (expects the PDFs above in a local folder D); crop boxes listed below.

## Primary documents
- [IND] Sealed Indictment, Dkt. 1, filed Dec 9, 2022 — https://archive.org/download/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940.1.0.pdf — Counts One–Two text, "proprietary crypto hedge fund", caption (img/indictment.jpg)
- [LET] U.S. Attorney's letter to Judge Kaplan, Dkt. 388, Dec 29, 2023 — https://archive.org/download/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940.388.0.pdf — original indictment Dec 9, 2022 in eight counts; Count Eight (campaign contributions) not tried because The Bahamas did not consent; extradited Dec 2022; S5 additional counts not tried (img/letter.jpg)
- [MEMO] Government sentencing memorandum, Dkt. 410, Mar 15, 2024 — https://archive.org/download/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940.410.0.pdf — MIT education (p.2); FTX ad "safest and easiest" and terms of service quote (p.5–6); Ellison "a good source of capital…" (Tr. 654); North Dimension accounts; "allow negative", $65B line of credit, exemption from auto-liquidation (p.7); Binance buyback exchange (Tr. 668) (p.9); venture spending incl. >$100M Bahamas real estate and $30M penthouse (p.10); June 2022 spreadsheet −$11B / $13B borrowed; $6.5B repaid to lenders, almost 70% customer money; Sept 1, 2022 $13.7B; balcony conversation "a little short on deliverable" (Tr. 1407) (p.11–12); Nov 2, 2022 leaked balance sheet; Nov 7 tweets; Nov 6 internal doc "enough to process ~1/3 of remaining client assets"; Signal "small group chat" $3.9B of $12B, $8.1B shortfall (p.12–13); halted withdrawals; Nov 11, 2022 bankruptcy filings (p.14); 40–50 years requested; witness tampering while on bail (p.29–31)
- [TR] Sentencing transcript, Dkt. 426, Mar 28, 2024 (filed Apr 9, 2024) — https://archive.org/download/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940.426.0.pdf — losses $1.7B investors / $1.3B lenders / ~$8B customers (p.6); Bankman-Fried's statement (p.33–34); coin-flip testimony read by the Court (p.51–52); "leitmotif" and "not his to use" / "betting on expected value" (p.53); "never a word of remorse for the commission of terrible crimes" (p.54); "risk that this man will be in a position to do something very bad in the future… not a negligible risk at all… disabling him" (p.55); "evasive, hairsplitting, dodging questions" (p.57); "I've been doing this job for close to 30 years. I've never seen a performance quite like that." (p.57–58); 300 months (p.58); three perjury findings (p.10–11)
- [JUDG] Judgment in a Criminal Case, Dkt. 424, Mar 28, 2024 — https://archive.org/download/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940.424.0.pdf — found guilty on counts (S6)1–7; 300 months; 3 years supervised release; forfeiture $11,020,000,000 (p.1, 3, 4, 7)
- [NT] Memorandum and Order Denying Motion for a New Trial, Dkt. 595, Apr 28, 2026 — https://archive.org/download/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940.595.0.pdf — motion filed Feb 5, 2026; "newly discovered" witnesses; "wildly conspiratorial and entirely contradicted by the record"; claims that victims were compensated fully "inaccurate or misleading"
- [MAND] Second Circuit judgment/mandate, Dkt. 601, issued Aug 4, 2026 (judgment of Jun 12, 2026, AFFIRMED; Parker, Lee, Kahn) — https://archive.org/download/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940/gov.uscourts.nysd.590940.601.0.pdf
