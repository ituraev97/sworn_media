Terry Rozier gambling case explained: federal prosecutors allege the former Charlotte Hornets guard agreed to an approximately $100,000 bribe to leave a March 23, 2023 game in New Orleans early so others could bet his unders. Rozier has pleaded not guilty and says he was injured.
NBA betting scandal, Terry Rozier charges, Deniro Laster, Marves Fairley guilty plea, Damon Jones, sports bribery, honest services fraud — new filings from November 12–13, 2026, ahead of the February 8, 2027 trial in Brooklyn.
Photo: Keith Allison, CC BY-SA 2.0 via Wikimedia Commons (cropped).
Terry Rozier is charged in the Eastern District of New York (No. 25-cr-323) with wire fraud conspiracy, money laundering conspiracy, bribery in sporting contests and honest services wire fraud conspiracy. He pleaded not guilty and is presumed innocent unless and until proven guilty. Co-defendant Deniro Laster has also pleaded not guilty. Marves Fairley and Damon Jones have pleaded guilty.
